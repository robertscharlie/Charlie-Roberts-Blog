A 2D pool/billiards game built with Python and Pygame, made as my A Level Computer Science Non-Exam Assessment (NEA) project, completed for the 2025 exam series.

It implements full 8-ball pool rules — turn tracking, spots/stripes assignment, potting, fouls, and win detection — on top of a physics engine handling ball-to-ball and ball-to-wall collisions, friction, and restitution. There's a basic AI opponent that selects and takes shots on its own, plus Player vs Player, Player vs AI, and a free-play Sandbox mode.

The rest is a full menu system (mode select, options, pause, win screens), player/ball customisation, sound effects and music with volume controls, a shot power slider, and a blurred-background menu effect.

**Stack:** Python, Pygame, NumPy, OpenCV

**Status:** complete
